willowPoint

Nepal cricket, in one place. Follow fixtures and results, browse competitions and grounds, and get concise answers from the match records we have.

Live scores only appear after a recent source check. If the feed is unavailable, the app says so rather than showing an old score as live. Fixture and result data can change; check the linked original source where available.

Follows and saved items are stored on your device and, when available, in an anonymous Firebase session. Cross-device account sign-in and push alerts are not available yet.
