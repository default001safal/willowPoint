willowPoint

Your cricket, closer. A Nepal-first companion for verified live scores, fixtures, results, current stories, and sourced answers from Cue.

Records and sources

Live scores appear only after recent, independent source agreement. If verification is unavailable, the app says so rather than presenting an old score as live. Fixtures and editorial records are refreshed by the scheduled collection pipeline. Original publisher links are shown when supplied.

Your account

Follows and saved items are kept on this device and synced with Firebase Auth and Firestore. You can continue as a guest, link Google, or use email sign-in. Signing out leaves the current device preferences available.

Availability

Source outages can delay or temporarily withhold records. Cue answers use the records available in the app and can fall back to an on-device rules engine when the proxy is unreachable.
